# 1.2.0

- Food from 45 more mods now counts toward your diet, around 2,900 items in total. Among them: Croptopia, Pam's HarvestCraft 2, Pantry for Blockheads, the Farmer's Delight add-on family, Alex's Caves, Alex's Mobs, Twilight Forest, The Undergarden, Eternal Starlight, Aquaculture, MineColonies, Nature's Spirit, Vinery and Farm & Charm.
- A cooked dish now counts toward the nutrients that actually went into it, in the proportions its recipe uses. A chicken sandwich splits between grain, protein and vegetable rather than giving full credit for each one.
- Farmer's Delight foods were rebalanced the same way. A few were plainly wrong before: bone broth counted as fruit, and kelp rolls counted as pure vegetable despite being mostly rice.
- Bottled drinks such as milk bottles are recognised as food now, so they count toward dairy instead of being skipped.
- Because mixed meals split their value across nutrients, the extra-nutrient penalty now applies on top of that split. If meals feel too weak on your server, lower `loss_per_extra_nutrient` in the `nutrition` section of `nutritional-server.toml`.
