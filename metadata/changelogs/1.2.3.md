# 1.2.3

- The diet tier widget now shows for 10 seconds when your tier changes, then fades out until the next change.
- While it's showing, a "Hotkey N" line under the widget reminds you which key opens the diet screen. It uses whatever key you've bound, and doesn't appear if the key is unbound.
- If you'd rather keep the widget on screen all the time, open the diet screen, hit **Move HUD** and turn on **Always Show**. You can also set `hud_always_show` in the `ui` section of `nutritional-client.toml`.
